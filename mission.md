Our mission is to promote and advance the sport of Pickleball on Bowen Island as an inclusive, accessible and enjoyable activity for people of all ages, abilities, and backgrounds. To increase community participation in physical activity by offering programming that removes barriers to entry and promotes broad, equitable access to Pickle Ball.
Our Current Mission is to see that the Bowen Island Community School (BICS) outdoor tennis court is resurfaced, repainted and made safe for not only tennis, but pickleball as well. A true multi-sport court.

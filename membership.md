# Memberships are now available

Memberships in the Bowen Island Pickleball Society are now available. They are at a reduced rate, as we are part way through the season. Click on the link to register and support Pickleball on Bowen, in BC and in Canada: [BIPS Memberships](https://us.list-manage.com/Ex4aFCFHPqW?e=6d62c6ee4e&c2id=4b8f02fd02437b2573b3668ad3cfa8dc)

**Good question, what do you get for your membership? Normally $75, now $45.**

You get hosted drop in play, with organized round robin play, balls to play with and a paddle if need be. We will host skills and drills, clinics for learning, coaching sessions, learn to play, an organized tournament, opportunities for kids play, volunteering, hosted evening socials at the pub, and lastly access to extra insurance from Pickleball Canada for tournaments ([Pickleball Canada &amp; BIPS](https://us.list-manage.com/_NHwVFwUT9n?e=6d62c6ee4e&c2id=4b8f02fd02437b2573b3668ad3cfa8dc)).

Members will be added to a BIPS WhatsApp group, where they will hear about events or cancellations.

Bowen Island Pickleball Society is a non-profit society, a collection of enthusiastic like minded individuals who love to play the game. It's not a matter of how good you are, how young or how old you are, it's a matter of having fun, laughing and exercising without really even realizing that you are exercising. It's a social society. An inclusive society. A society for active aging and turning a good day into a great day!

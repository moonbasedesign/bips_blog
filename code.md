#BOWEN ISLAND PICKLEBALL SOCIETY 

* Maintain and enhance the dignity and self-esteem of members and non-members by: 
    Demonstrating respect for every person regardless of physical characteristics, athletic ability, age, colour, race, citizenship, ethnic origin, creed, disability, economic or marital status, gender identity or expression, or sexual orientation. 
*   Focusing comments or criticism appropriately and avoiding public criticism of athletes, coaches, officials, organizers, volunteers, employees or members.
* Consistently demonstrating the spirit of sportsmanship, sport leadership and ethical conduct. 
* Acting, when appropriate, to correct or prevent practices that are unjust and discriminatory. 
* Consistently treating individuals fairly and reasonably. 
* Ensuring adherence to the rules of pickleball and the spirit of those rules

* Refrain from: 
    Verbal or physical abuse. 
    Any form of harassment, including sexual harassment. 
    Using of profane, insulting or otherwise offensive language. 
* Using of power or authority to coerce another person. 
Consuming alcohol, tobacco products or recreational drugs while participating in BIPS activities. 

* Abstain from: 
*   Offering coaching tips without being asked 
*   Publicly speaking poorly of club officials or volunteers
*   Manipulating club playing structures (like Drop In Play or Hold My Court) to create a personally beneficial outcome. 
*   Respect the property of others and not willfully cause damage. 

* Comply, always, with BIPS bylaws, policies, procedures, rules and regulations, as adopted and amended from time to time. 

* Conduct themselves in a manner that reflects the highest standard of behaviour arising within the business, activities or events of the Bowen Island Pickleball Society. 

* Approve of and adopt the BIPS council’s guiding questions in decision making:
    What is in the best interest of the BIPS?
    Is there an opportunity to create a win-win?
    How would the other person want to be treated in this situation?
